# Skincare Routine Rules & Knowledge Base Schema (`docs/ROUTINE_RULES.md`)

## General Medical Disclaimer
The skincare routines generated by GlowAI are for general educational and cosmetic guidance. They do not constitute medical advice or a formal diagnosis. Always consult a board-certified dermatologist for persistent, severe, or painful skin conditions.

## Knowledge Base Schema (`assets/data/routine_rules.json`)

The routine generator uses a JSON-driven schema:
- `version`: Version string of the rule set.
- `disclaimer`: Regulatory disclaimer text.
- `base_steps`: Keyed by skin type (`Oily`, `Dry`, `Combination`, `Normal`), containing `AM` and `PM` step arrays.
  - `id`: Unique step identifier (e.g. `step_cleanser_am`).
  - `title`: Step name.
  - `category`: Category (`Cleanser`, `Toner`, `Serum`, `Moisturizer`, `Sunscreen`, `Night Treatment`).
  - `stepNumber`: 1 to 5.
  - `session`: `AM` or `PM`.
  - `texture`: Recommended product texture.
  - `keyIngredients`: Target active ingredients.
  - `howToApply`: Array of numbered application micro-steps.
  - `whyItHelps`: Rationale for this step.
  - `avoidNotes`: Contraindications or warnings.
  - `waitTimeMinutes`: Recommended wait before next step.
  - `productCategory`: Category string for linking to catalog search.
- `weekly_extras`: Recommended 1-2x/week treatments per skin type.

## Safety & Exclusion Filters
1. **Pregnancy / Breastfeeding Flag:** Automatically filters out Retinoids (Retinol, Tretinoin, Adaptalene) and high-strength Salicylic Acid (>2%).
2. **Fragrance Allergy Flag:** Filters out products containing artificial fragrance.
3. **Sensitive Skin:** Replaces harsh physical exfoliants with soothing ingredients (Centella, Ceramides, Panthenol).
